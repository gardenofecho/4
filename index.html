Update log

Execution: compute at ~3:45 PM ET and trade with market-on-close orders (most brokers stop accepting MOC orders around 3:50 PM ET); next-morning execution degrades results. Uptrend = QQQ above its 200-day average and that average higher than 20 trading days earlier; anything else is a downtrend. Uptrend: TQQQ 10-day RSI above 79 → 100% cash. Otherwise TQQQ = 0.6 ÷ (3 × QQQ 20-day annualized volatility), rounded to the nearest 25% and capped at 100%; halved when on-balance volume is below its 50-day EMA (OBV uses QQQ volume as a proxy for the Nasdaq-100). The rest sits in cash / T-bills. Downtrend: RSI below 31 → 100% TQQQ; otherwise TQQQ below its 20-day average → 50% SQQQ / 50% cash; otherwise 100% cash. Rebalance when the target changes or any holding drifts more than 5 percentage points. Leveraged ETFs can lose most of their value in a drawdown.
